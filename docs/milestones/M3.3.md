# Milestone M3.3 — normal-validation evaluation

M3.3 evaluates the frozen R0 baseline and all 24 previously trained LightGBM task predictors on the normal-policy validation window, April 30–May 2, 2022. It does not retrain a model, fit a validation vocabulary, inspect randomized-validation outcomes, or access May 3–8 outcomes.

## Validation features

The validation population contains 61,548 unique, canonically ordered impressions. FS1, FS2, and FS3 use the frozen feature definitions and exactly match their training schemas. Labels are stored separately and align position by position through `impression_id`.

Point-in-time aggregate and affinity features draw exclusively from normal-policy history. For a target on day `d`, as-of joins use only dates less than `d`; history is loaded through May 1 so May 2 features may use May 1 normal interactions. Same-day and future outcomes cannot contribute. The history loader separately authorizes Apr 9–29 training history and Apr 30–May 1 normal-validation history; it has no randomized source path. FS3 recencies use the same prior-day cutoff. No final-test feature is generated.

Validation artifacts are under `~/recom/processed/validation/v1/`. The three stages consume 11.22 MiB including labels and manifests.

## Scoring and selection

Every LightGBM bundle is loaded with its M3.2b checksum, frozen M2 feature-manifest hash, feature-set/task/depth compatibility checks, and saved training adapter. Unknown validation categories map to the saved unknown code; no vocabulary is fitted or extended. Model files are neither modified nor retrained.

Predictions are under `~/recom/predictions/validation/v1/` and occupy 8.86 MiB including manifests and machine-readable results. Prediction tables contain only canonical impression IDs and scores. The task masks remain:

- long view and like: all 61,548 rows;
- valid play: 47,341 tab-1 rows;
- literal click: 5,095 tab-0 rows.

The frozen selection rule is maximum normal-validation long-view pair-weighted within-user AUC among LightGBM candidates. It selects **FS1 deep**, with 0.655549. This selection is recorded for later RQ2 work. It does not compare LightGBM against untrained neural architectures and is not a final randomized-test result.

## Reproduction

```bash
source .venv/bin/activate
python scripts/evaluate_normal_validation.py
pytest -q
```

Detailed results and limitations are in `reports/m3_3_normal_validation.md`; machine-readable metrics are in `~/recom/predictions/validation/v1/validation_results.json`.
