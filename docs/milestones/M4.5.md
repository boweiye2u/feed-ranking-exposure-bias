# M4.5 — Neural normal-validation evaluation and candidate freeze

M4.5 scores the 15 immutable neural checkpoints on the authorized normal-policy validation interval, April 30–May 2, 2022. It does not retrain a model or refit preprocessing. The training-fitted neural adapter at `~/recom/models/neural/infrastructure/v1/preprocessor.json` is reused and verified against the frozen M2 feature-manifest hash.

## Validation inputs

- FS3 contains 61,548 impressions in ascending `impression_id` order.
- Labels are read only from the authorized normal-validation table and remain separate from model inputs.
- The neural sequence table contains exactly 20 left-padded positions per row. Its source is normal-policy structural impressions from April 9 through May 2; every retained event satisfies `history_time_ms < target_time_ms`.
- Randomized interactions are not a history source. Sequence construction does not read outcome columns.
- The training vocabularies, unknown IDs, imputation values, scaling values, and feature selector are reused unchanged. `context__is_rand` remains excluded.

The sequence artifact is stored at `~/recom/processed/validation/v1/fs3/normal_validation/sequence.parquet`. Neural predictions and machine-readable results are stored beneath `~/recom/predictions/validation/v1/`.

## Evaluation population and metrics

Each shared-bottom, full-MMoE, and predefined MMoE-ablation checkpoint is scored for seeds 0, 1, and 2. The tasks are long view and like on all 61,548 rows, and valid play on the 47,341 tab-1 rows. Literal click is not a neural head.

Metrics reuse the frozen M3.3 implementation: pair-weighted within-user AUC (primary), equal-user AUC, 952-pair-capped AUC, pooled AUC, binary LogLoss, and NDCG@5. Score ties receive 0.5 concordance credit. Seed 0 is the prespecified neural selection seed; seeds 1 and 2 are robustness runs and are not averaged for selection.

## Frozen candidate population

`~/recom/predictions/validation/v1/candidate_manifest.json` freezes exactly 12 configurations: R0; LightGBM FS1/FS2/FS3 × shallow/deep; shared-bottom; full MMoE; and the dense-only, no-history, and no-aggregates MMoE ablations. ESMM is excluded. The manifest records paths and hashes, preprocessing identity, training settings, metric definition, seed rule, and the selected normal-validation configuration. All artifacts are retained.

## Reproduction

```bash
python scripts/evaluate_neural_validation.py
pytest -q
```

The script requires the previously built normal-validation FS3 tables and frozen M3/M4 model bundles. It never builds final-test features or reads randomized-validation or final-test outcomes.
