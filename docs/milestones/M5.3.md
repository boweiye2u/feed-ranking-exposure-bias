# M5.3 — Pre-final artifact and inference freeze

M5.3 verifies all frozen model artifacts and prepares final-evaluation contracts without materializing final features or opening held-out outcomes.

The machine-readable readiness files are:

- `~/recom/processed/prefinal/v1/contender_manifest.json`
- `~/recom/processed/prefinal/v1/readiness_manifest.json`

The complete RQ1/RQ2 population remains the 12 configurations frozen at M4.5. Neural seed 0 is primary; seeds 1 and 2 remain robustness runs. FS1-deep LightGBM remains the normal-validation-selected workflow model. The authorized normal-validation contender freeze contains `fs1_deep`, `fs1_shallow`, and `mmoe_no_aggregates`.

The final feature contract covers T-normal and T-random on May 3–8. Aggregate histories are strictly prior-calendar-day normal-policy events; last-20 histories are strictly earlier normal-policy impressions. Training-fitted adapters must be reused without vocabulary fitting. The joint RQ3 arm sets `context__is_rand=1` for T-random. Prefinal requests containing outcome columns or requesting held-out outcomes fail closed.

Inference definitions, including the approved M5.3 amendment, are encoded in `configs/final_evaluation.toml`. The implementation precomputes user-level concordance and pair counts, supports paired multinomial user bootstraps, cross-fitted regret, percentile intervals, tail-share p-values, and standard Holm adjustment.

Reproduce the outcome-free readiness audit with:

```bash
python scripts/freeze_m5_prefinal.py
pytest -q
```

Do not invoke final outcome access or materialize final outputs until the separately authorized final-evaluation milestone.
